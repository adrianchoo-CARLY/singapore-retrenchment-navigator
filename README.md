# Singapore Responsible Retrenchment Navigator

**Built for retrenchment exercises in Singapore. Usable elsewhere as general guidance.**

A diagnostic for HR leaders, business owners and management teams who are planning a retrenchment exercise affecting employees in Singapore. Claude asks a few short multiple-choice questions about your biggest concern, then gives you one advisory card that names the most likely risk, explains why it matters, and sets out the one thing to do next.

Built by Career Agility International from its *Responsible Retrenchment* handbook and long experience supporting employers and employees through restructuring in Singapore.

> **General guidance only. This is not legal advice.** Verify current requirements with MOM, TAFEP, WSG, IRAS and qualified advisors before making decisions.

## Scope: built for Singapore

Every question and card is built on Singapore law and practice:

- the Employment Act and MOM requirements, including mandatory retrenchment notification
- the Tripartite Advisory on Managing Excess Manpower and Responsible Retrenchment
- TAFEP guidance on fair and responsible retrenchment
- WSG support for affected employees and IRAS tax treatment of retrenchment benefits
- work pass rules for foreign employees in Singapore (EP, S Pass, Work Permit and dependants)

You can still use it for a retrenchment outside Singapore. Most of the guidance on readiness, fair selection, communication, Notification Day and supporting leavers and stayers applies anywhere. Claude will tell you that the advice is written for the Singapore context, so use it judiciously and check with your local authorities and a qualified employment lawyer. Questions and cards that rest on Singapore-only rules, such as MOM notification and work passes, are flagged so you can check the local equivalent.

## What it covers

The navigator starts with five concerns and routes you through up to nine short questions to one of 42 advisory cards:

| Starting concern | What it checks |
| --- | --- |
| Are we ready to retrench? | Alternatives considered, the right exit route, timeline, technology-driven change |
| Are we choosing the right people? | Objective criteria, fairness risk, cost as a proxy for age or tenure, performance records |
| Who do we need to inform? | MOM retrenchment notification, early alerts, union consultation, communication sequence |
| What package and support should we offer? | Severance benchmarking, eligibility, items beyond severance, outplacement, foreign employees on work passes |
| How do we handle the exit itself? | Notification Day run-sheet, who delivers the message, emotional reactions, reputation, the stayers |

Each card has four parts: **Where you may be**, **Why this matters**, **The one thing to do now**, and **The question your leadership team should answer**.

## How to use it

Install the plugin, then start a conversation in Claude. The skill loads when you describe a retrenchment, layoff or redundancy exercise in Singapore, or when you ask for it by name. If it is not clear where the affected employees work, Claude asks first. Answer each question with the option number, the option text, or your own words.

### Example prompts

1. "Run the Singapore Responsible Retrenchment Navigator."
2. "We need to cut 15 roles in our Singapore office next month. Where are our biggest risks?"
3. "I'm worried our retrenchment list is heavy on older, long-serving staff. Can you help me check our selection?"
4. "Do we need to notify MOM, and who else should we tell before we announce the layoffs?"
5. "How should our managers handle Notification Day?"

## What it runs, sends and stores

- **Runs:** nothing. The plugin is one skill (instructions for Claude) and one JSON data file holding the questions and advisory cards. It has no scripts, hooks, MCP servers or commands.
- **Sends:** nothing. It makes no network calls and does not connect to any external service. Links to official sources are shown for you to open yourself.
- **Stores:** nothing. It keeps no record of your answers. Your conversation stays in your own Claude account, governed by your Claude settings.
- **Asks you not to share** names, NRIC or FIN numbers, salaries or other personal details of individual employees. The diagnostic only needs answers about the exercise as a whole.
- **Gives general guidance only.** It does not decide whether an exercise is lawful or compliant.

## Troubleshooting

- **The navigator does not start.** Ask for it by name: "Run the Singapore Responsible Retrenchment Navigator."
- **Claude misread my answer.** Reply with the option number, or say "go back" to change an earlier answer.
- **My situation is not in Singapore.** The navigator still runs. Treat it as general guidance, and check the flagged Singapore-specific points with your local authorities.
- **We operate in several countries.** The navigator runs for the whole exercise and flags the points that apply only to your Singapore employees.
- **I want to explore another area.** After a card, choose "explore another concern" to start again.
